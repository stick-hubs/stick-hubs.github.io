SwiftNet

Web browser · version 1.0.1 · NEW

A fast web browser built on Chromium, the same engine as Chrome. It has tabs, bookmarks, history, a downloads bar, find on page and zoom, and opens Stick Hubs when you start it.

  • Two looks: Windows 7 Aero glass, or Modern. Switch any time in the menu.
  • Claude panel: chat with Claude next to any page. Press Ask about this page and the page (or the text you selected) goes into Claude's message box, so you can ask about it. Works with a free claude.ai account.
  • Private: SwiftNet itself has no ads and doesn't track you. Your history and bookmarks stay on your computer.
  • Update button: SwiftNet tells you when a new version is out.
WindowsMac (Apple chip)Mac (Intel): coming later

Windows: no install needed, just open it. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. Mac: unzip, drag SwiftNet to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open the first time.

Swiss

Search engine inside Stick Hubs · free

A calm, simple search engine right inside Stick Hubs, for everyone, including people who don't play games. Big clear text and no clutter. It has its own robot, SwissBot, that reads safe websites every day, plus quick answers for weather, maps, word meanings, books and sums. Nothing to download.
